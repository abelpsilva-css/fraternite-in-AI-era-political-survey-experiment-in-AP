#Fraternitè in AI era: survey experiment on AP in Catalonia (Spain)
## fraternite-in-AI-era-political-survey-experiment-in-AP
Affective polarization reinforces epistemic bubbles. While AI verification labels have limited success in two-party systems, their effect in multi-party systems is unexplored (multiple cleavages). This survey experiment in Catalonia tests whether Grok AI fact-checking on in/out-party tweets reduces polarizing attitudes among youth (18–35) in Barcelona (original work is writen in Spanish).
